Dapur Kilat v2 — a polished rebuild of the Indonesian warung cooking game for the make.fun best cooking game bounty. One cook, one 150-second shift: grab ingredients from the wall bins, grill meat, chop vegetables, assemble plates on the serving counter, and serve orders before their patience runs out. 8 ingredients, 6 recipes, speed bonuses and combo multipliers, best score saved locally.

This v2 fixes the first entry's two visual weaknesses: the title screen is now a slim banner at the top so the kitchen and the dish mid-assembly stay fully visible on the cover, and the art is richer — warm gradient lighting, soft shadows, textured floors, a glowing grill with steam, and a more detailed chef.

Controls: WASD / arrows to walk, E to use a station, Space / Enter to start, P / Esc to pause, click to walk (click a station to walk there and use it).

Run locally with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
